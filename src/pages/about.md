Hi, I’m Ryan. I build data systems and collaborate on research at the Federal
Reserve Bank of Boston. This site collects my notes,
[projects](/#selected-work), and [technical writing](/posts/).

My background is in computer science and statistics. Most of my work involves
building reliable software and data systems, automating complex workflows, and
developing applied AI/ML tools.

[View my résumé](https://resume.ryancswallace.dev) or
[email me](mailto:ryan@ryancswallace.dev).

## What I Do

- **Data systems:** pipelines and interfaces for datasets that change over time
- **Machine learning and NLP:** model development and evaluation for applied
  problems
- **Research tools:** reproducible workflows and automation for long-running
  workloads
- **Developer tools:** practical defaults that remove repetitive setup and make
  projects easier to maintain

## Snapshot

- **Location:** Boston, MA
- **Background:** Computer Science and Statistics at Harvard; data science and
  engineering at the Federal Reserve Bank of Boston
- **Current work:** Data systems, applied AI/ML, and research infrastructure
- **Interests:** Distributed systems, databases, Linux, and developer tooling
